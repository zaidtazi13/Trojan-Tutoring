Academic Interview Coaching with Dr Rachid Tazi

Prepare with the insight of an experienced medical interviewer.

Advanced medical interview coaching drawing on Dr Rachid Tazi’s experience in medical education, university admissions and interviewing hundreds of prospective medical students.

£100 per sessionDBS-checked tutorsSafeguarding trained

Dr Rachid’s interview programme

  • The programme begins with a comprehensive introduction to the MMI format: structure, expectations, assessment criteria and the most common themes seen across UK medical schools.
  • Although stations vary between universities and from year to year, preparation focuses on the core themes and station types that recur across medical interviews.
  • Subsequent sessions are fully personalised using the candidate’s experiences, achievements and individual strengths, so they learn how to apply their own evidence to a wide range of scenarios.
  • Dr Rachid provides interviewer-level feedback on the qualities medical schools look for beyond grades, including communication, critical thinking, empathy, ethical reasoning and professionalism.
  • For many candidates, around three to four focused sessions can provide comprehensive preparation, with additional sessions available where needed.

What candidates should be able to do

  • Understand the MMI format, expectations and assessment criteria.
  • Recognise core MMI themes and common station types.
  • Apply their own experiences and strengths effectively to different scenarios.
  • Give confident, structured answers to challenging questions.
  • Demonstrate empathy, ethical reasoning, critical thinking and professionalism.
  • Approach stations with greater adaptability and a clearer understanding of what interviewers are assessing.

Progress tracking between sessions

  • Tutors keep note of recurring interview weak points, feedback themes and areas that need more practice so preparation remains personalised.
  • Previous weak areas are revisited in later sessions to check that feedback has been applied and that responses, communication and confidence are improving.

What a session looks like

This is not a generic question bank. Coaching is designed around the individual candidate and uses interviewer-style challenge, follow-up questioning and detailed feedback to refine judgement, communication and the ability to articulate personal experiences effectively.

Quality & safeguarding: all tutors are DBS checked and safeguarding trained. With student consent, sessions may be recorded for quality assurance so teaching standards can be reviewed and maintained.
