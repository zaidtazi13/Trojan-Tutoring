Tutoring Programme

Tutoring Format

The programme begins with a comprehensive introductory session covering the MMI format, including the structure, expectations and most common themes encountered across medical school interview stations.

It is important to recognise that MMI stations can vary between medical schools and may change from year to year. However, there are several core themes and station types that are commonly assessed across UK medical schools. These form the foundation of our preparation programme.

Subsequent sessions are fully personalised to each candidate, using their CV, experiences, achievements and individual strengths as a basis for preparation. Candidates will learn how to draw upon their own experiences and effectively apply them to a wide range of MMI scenarios.

The number of sessions required will depend on the individual candidate, their experience and the medical schools they are applying to. For most candidates, three to four focused sessions should provide comprehensive preparation, with additional sessions available where required.

Learning Outcomes

  1. Understand the MMI format — including its structure, expectations and assessment criteria.
  2. Understand the core MMI themes and station types commonly encountered across UK medical schools.
  3. Apply their own experiences and strengths effectively to different MMI scenarios.
  4. Develop confident and structured communication when responding to challenging interview questions.
  5. Demonstrate key qualities such as empathy, ethical reasoning, critical thinking and professionalism.
  6. Approach MMI stations with greater confidence, adaptability and a clear understanding of what interviewers are assessing.

Building the foundations for everything that comes next.

Over the last two years of teaching KS3 students, I have come to realise just how important these years are in a child’s education. This is often the stage where confidence is built, students begin to understand their own learning styles, and their attitudes towards different subjects start to develop. Clear progress for parents · Parent Progress Portal

See what your child is learning, where they are progressing and what needs more work.

Parents can follow their child’s tutoring progress in one clear place. After each lesson, tutors can record what was covered, strengths, weaker areas and next steps, giving families a lesson-by-lesson view of how learning is developing and what will be revisited.

01

Access your portal

Once set up with Trojan Tutoring, families can use their progress portal to keep lesson information organised in one place, including schedules and shared lesson information.

02

Read each lesson update

After each session, the tutor can record what was taught, the student’s progress and the key areas that still need attention. This gives parents a clear record of what their child has worked through.

03

Track weak points over time

Trojan tutors will use their lesson records to carry weaker topics forward. Parents can follow which areas have been identified and see when they are revisited through recaps, retrieval and further practice.

How progress tracking works for Trojan families

Our secure student and parent portal where lesson notes and resources can be shared and families can view lesson information outside of sessions. Trojan Tutoring will use these features to make progress more transparent: your tutor records the learning covered and areas requiring further work, and those notes build into a useful lesson-by-lesson history rather than progress disappearing once a session ends.

Important: The portal provides the record-keeping tools; your Trojan tutor is responsible for identifying and recording the student’s individual strengths, weak points and next steps.
