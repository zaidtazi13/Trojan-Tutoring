Interactive Group Seminars

Focused small-group support across subjects and applications.

Small, interactive seminars for KS3, GCSE and A-Level subjects, as well as Medicine and Dentistry application support. Each seminar is limited to a maximum of 5 students, giving everyone space for focused teaching, discussion, questions and guidance.

How our seminars work

  • Each seminar is limited to a maximum of 5 students to keep the session genuinely interactive and give everyone opportunities to participate.
  • Each seminar has a clear topic and set of learning goals so students know what will be covered before joining.
  • The tutor teaches the key ideas first, then works through examples and questions with the group.
  • Students are encouraged to answer, ask questions and compare approaches rather than passively watch.
  • Common mistakes are discussed openly so everyone benefits from the questions raised by the group.

What we want to help with

  • KS3, GCSE and A-Level subject support across relevant topics and exam content.
  • Medicine and Dentistry application support, including key admissions themes and preparation.
  • Exam-question technique, discussion and focused revision.
  • Learning from other students’ questions and approaches in a small group.
  • Getting focused support at a lower cost than one-to-one tuition.

What a session looks like

Seminars are designed to feel like an interactive workshop rather than a lecture. The exact content varies by the advertised subject, year group or Medicine and Dentistry application theme.

Quality & safeguarding: all tutors are DBS checked and safeguarding trained. With student consent, sessions may be recorded for quality assurance so teaching standards can be reviewed and maintained.

How the parent progress portal works

A clear record of each lesson, not just a general progress update.

Once your child begins tuition, parents can be given access to the secure progress portal used by Trojan Tutoring. The portal acts as one place to follow the tuition taking place between lessons.

  1. After the lesson, the tutor records the session. This includes the main topics covered, what the student understood well and any areas where they struggled.
  2. Weak points are identified and carried forward. If a topic is not yet secure, it is recorded so the next tutor session can revisit it through recap, retrieval questions or further exam practice.
  3. Parents can follow the history over time. Rather than relying on occasional verbal updates, you can see what has been taught from lesson to lesson and how previously identified weaknesses are developing.
  4. Next steps stay visible. The tutor can use the previous lesson record to plan what should be reinforced, practised or progressed next.

Important: The portal is used to organise and share the information. Your Trojan tutor is the person who assesses the student’s strengths, identifies weak points and decides what needs to be revisited.
