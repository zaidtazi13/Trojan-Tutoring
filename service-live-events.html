Live Q&A Events

Get clear answers to the questions students ask most.

Live events give students direct access to tutors for commonly submitted questions, admissions topics and recurring academic difficulties.

See event listingDBS-checked tutorsSafeguarding trained

How live Q&A works

  • Questions can be submitted in advance where advertised, allowing tutors to prepare useful examples and explanations.
  • The tutor works through the most common or valuable questions live and explains the reasoning behind each answer.
  • Students can ask follow-up questions so unclear points can be explored further.
  • Where appropriate, sessions include practical examples, exam questions or admissions scenarios.

What we want to help with

  • Questions that do not require a full one-to-one lesson.
  • Common exam, UCAT or admissions uncertainties.
  • Clarifying confusing topics before tests or application milestones.
  • Giving students access to practical advice in an interactive format.

What a session looks like

Each event has its own focus, so students should check the advertised topic and year group before joining.

Quality & safeguarding: all tutors are DBS checked and safeguarding trained. With student consent, sessions may be recorded for quality assurance so teaching standards can be reviewed and maintained.
