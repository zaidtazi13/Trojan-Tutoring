HOW PROGRESS TRACKING WORKS

Your child’s tutor uses each lesson to build the next one. Our parent portal helps us keep that process visible to parents.

01

Lesson takes place

The tutor teaches the planned topic, checks understanding and notices where the student is secure or still struggling.

02

Tutor records the lesson

After the session, the tutor adds useful notes to the parent portal: what was covered, what went well, weak points, and what should be reinforced next.

03

Parents review the update

Parents can review the lesson history so they know what their child has actually worked through and where support is still needed.

04

Weak points carry forward

Identified gaps are not forgotten. The tutor uses previous notes to revisit them through recap, retrieval practice, questions or further teaching.

WHY THIS MATTERS TO US

Keeping parents part of the progress

At Trojan Tutoring, we believe parents should have a clear understanding of how their child is progressing. Tutoring should not feel like a closed door where parents only hear an occasional update. Our progress portal keeps parents informed about what is being covered, where their child is improving and which areas still need attention, creating greater transparency and helping everyone work towards the same goals.
